File: `src/lib/types.ts`

Purpose and responsibilities: Defines shared domain vocabulary for teams, players, SOLENIZANT, The Floor, SPORT, Jaka to melodia?, Pojedynki, snapshots, and verified sessions.

Important exports, inputs, and outputs: The Floor duel contract contains two team IDs, the active team, team clocks, a winning team, bet count, and only the current viewer's own team bet outcome. SPORT adds persisted pairing/rank/challenge/bonus contracts. Melodia exposes song and reward data, while Pojedynki exposes its duel and viewer-bet contracts. Chip values remain decimal strings.

Dependencies: None.

Backend/database interactions: Models safe state returned by `database.ts`; it does not access persistence.

Implementation details: The Floor uses teams as the competing sides. SPORT pair slots A/B are resolved from administrator-selected teams; the compatibility-named `SportPlayerNumber.number` field is a positive player rank. Ranks 1–5 identify scheduled participants, while higher ranks remain valid roster metadata. Individual players remain roster members and bettors, while a SPORT challenge winner is always a team.

Future expansion: Add fields only after their persistent and API behavior is defined.

Segment V models the confirmed-team stage, five song slots, lyric-auction details, team totals, final leaders, selected winner, settlement marker, range of per-player debits, and the viewer's own recorded balance delta. Chip values are exact decimal strings. Previously settled legacy results carry an archive flag.

Segment VI update: `SegmentId` additionally includes `pojedynki`. `PojedynkiState` and `PojedynkiDuel` expose only the four durable slots, selected team sides, stage, result, aggregate bet count, and the requesting player's isolated bet outcome. `GameState` holds its independent stage and zero-based current-duel sequence.

Segment VIII adds `familiada` to `SegmentId` and `FamiliadaSnapshot` to the shared game snapshot. It carries an opaque revision, match score summaries, current phase, the current ranked board, representatives and the viewer's private wager. An unrevealed slot carries only its position and null. The optional current answer key is populated by authorized administrator reads.

The Floor snapshot content carries the active category name, instructions, item count, a Polish category error if applicable, and the current typed text/image/error prompt during play.
