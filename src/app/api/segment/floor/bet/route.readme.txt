File: `src/app/api/segment/floor/bet/route.ts`

Purpose and responsibilities: Records one fixed-stake player bet on a competing The Floor team.

Important exports, inputs, and outputs: Player-only `POST(request)` validates `{ targetTeamId }` and returns `{ ok: true }` or Polish errors.

Dependencies: Next Route Handler APIs, team-bet validation, opaque sessions, and `database.ts`.

Backend/database interactions: Uses the verified session player ID, not a browser-supplied bettor. The database checks the active betting stage, target-team membership, bettor's team eligibility, duplicate bet, and balance before atomically debiting and inserting the bet.

Implementation details: Every member of the two competing teams is excluded; the amount is server-owned and cannot be changed by the browser.

Nowe rundy rezerwują 4 żetony. Otwarte przed zmianą stawki rundy korzystają z wcześniejszej kwoty.
